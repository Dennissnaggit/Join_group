Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 16, 2026

1. About this privacy policy

This privacy policy explains how personal data is processed when you use Join, a task-management application created as an educational project. Join allows users to create an account, manage contacts and organize tasks on a board. Please do not enter sensitive personal information or use the application for confidential business data.

3. Data we process

Depending on how you use Join, the following categories of data may be processed:

  • Account data: name, email address, authentication identifier and login status.
  • Contact data: names, email addresses, telephone numbers, colors and other information you enter for contacts.
  • Task data: titles, descriptions, due dates, categories, priorities, assignments, subtasks and completion status.
  • Technical data: information required to deliver and secure the service, such as timestamps, browser requests, device or connection information and error data.
  • Local app data: session information, the displayed user name and guest-mode tasks or contacts stored in your browser.

4. Purposes and legal bases

We process data to create and authenticate accounts, provide the task and contact management functions, save your changes, maintain login sessions, prevent misuse and diagnose technical problems. Where the GDPR applies, processing necessary to provide the requested application functions is based on Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. Security, error prevention and reliable operation are based on our legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. Where consent is specifically requested, processing is based on Article 6(1)(a) GDPR and consent may be withdrawn at any time with effect for the future.

5. Firebase services

Join uses Google Firebase, provided in Europe by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. Firebase Authentication is used for account registration and login. Cloud Firestore is used to store user profiles, contacts and tasks. Google processes this data on our behalf to provide, secure and maintain these services. Depending on service configuration and support requirements, data may also be processed outside the European Economic Area. Google provides contractual safeguards for relevant international transfers.

More information is available in the Firebase privacy and security information and the Firebase Data Processing and Security Terms.

6. Browser storage

Join uses local storage and session storage in your browser to keep essential application state, such as login-related display information, the completion state of the login animation and guest-mode data. This information remains on your device until it is removed by the application, you log out where applicable, or you clear the browser storage. Join does not use advertising or analytics cookies.

7. Recipients

Personal data is available only to the extent necessary to the project operator and technical service providers used to run the application, particularly Google Firebase. Data is not sold and is not shared with advertisers. Information you add about another person should only be entered when you are authorized to do so.

8. Retention and deletion

Account, contact and task data is retained for as long as the relevant account is active or the data is needed to provide Join. Individual contacts and tasks may be deleted through the available application functions. Local guest data remains in the browser until it is cleared. Data may be retained for longer where required to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes or protect legal claims. To request deletion of an account and associated data, contact the controller using the details above.

9. Your rights

Subject to the applicable legal requirements, you may have the right to request access to and rectification or deletion of your personal data, restriction of processing, data portability, and to object to processing based on legitimate interests. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw that consent at any time. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a competent data protection supervisory authority.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, loss or disclosure. Data transmitted between your browser and Firebase is protected using encrypted connections. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so users should choose a strong, unique password and keep their login credentials confidential.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy when the application, service providers or legal requirements change. The current version and its update date will always be published on this page.
